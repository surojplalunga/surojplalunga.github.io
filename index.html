Suroj Plalunga

BSc Mathematics · Tribhuvan University, Nepal

My name is Suroj Plalunga. I am a BSc Mathematics student at Tribhuvan University, Nepal.

I split my time between proofs and terminals — Graph theory, real analysis and topology on one side of the desk, a heavily customized Arch Linux + Hyprland setup on the other.

My daily driver is Neovim (Native Package Manger Vim.Pack ) with tinymist for Typst, Zathura for bidirectionally-synced PDF preview, and tmux holding it all together.

// currently reading Discrete math, Real Analysis and Point set Topology - July 2026.

## interested in

Algebraic geometry, formal methods (Lean 4), symbolic computation, and the design of Mathematical tools.
